Permanent poly-cotton name labels you type, print and press in your own facility. LTS-Labels do not fade out or fall off, and they outlast the clothing.

How it Works

3 steps to professionally labeled clothing!

  1. 1. Type

    Type your resident’s name in the software

    • Consists of one screen for ease of use
    • Database stores names for repeat printing
    • Delete function to remove old names
    • Easy to understand “drop down” menu
    • Pre-installed on computers purchased from LTS
    • Special pricing when purchased with LTS Laundry Labels

  2. 2. Print

    Print white poly-cotton labels on the impact printer

    The durable 24 pin Epson printer will print 100 labels in less than 2 minutes.

  3. 3. Press

    Apply labels with the Quick Press

    Our Quick Press will apply your labels in less than 6 seconds, less time than it takes to sort your resident’s laundry.
